How fresh orders work

Harvested, packed cold, shipped Monday.

  1. Order by Friday

    Fresh orders received by 6 p.m. Eastern on Friday make the next shipment.

  2. Ships Monday

    Packed with insulated cold packs and sent by USPS Priority Mail early in the week.

  3. Refrigerate

    Unpack on arrival. Rhizomes keep 2 to 3 weeks; plant starts go in the ground within a week.

  4. Grate or grow

    Grate just before serving, or plant your starts in cool shade.
