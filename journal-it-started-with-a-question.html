It started with a question

October 15, 2026

“I wonder if we can grow wasabi in the holler?” How a curious experiment turned into a farm.

Real wasabi wants deep shade, cool air and clean, cold water moving past its roots. Few places in the world offer all three. But anyone who has spent time in the mountains of Western North Carolina knows a place that sounds a lot like that: the holler.

So one day the question got asked out loud: I wonder if we can grow wasabi in the holler?

Finding out

We didn’t set out to start a farm. A few plants went into the ground beside a spring, and then we watched and learned as we went. Season by season, the patch filled in. Then it flowered.

From one holler to many

Today that experiment has grown into partnerships with landowners across several Western North Carolina counties, each site with its own spring, elevation and microclimate.

This journal is where we’ll share what happens next: harvest notes, growing lessons, recipes and news from the patch. Read the full story on our About page.
