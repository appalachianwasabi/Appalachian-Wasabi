Shipping

How we ship.

Fresh wasabi is perishable, so we pack it carefully and ship it quickly. Here is what to expect.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within the United States. If you are outside the U.S. and would like to order, send us a message and we will let you know whether we can help.

When orders ship

Most orders ship within 3 business days of purchase. To keep fresh products from sitting in a carrier’s facility over a weekend, we ship Monday through Wednesday. An order placed late in the week will ship the following Monday.

You will receive an email with tracking information when your order ships.

Shipping rates

Shipping is a flat rate that is shown at checkout before you pay. Any free-shipping offer will also be shown at checkout. Your shipping charge covers the whole order, so please check the total on the checkout page before you pay.

Carriers and delivery times

We ship with USPS, UPS or a similar carrier, chosen to get your order to you as quickly as possible. Delivery times are estimates from the carrier and are not guaranteed. Weather and carrier delays can happen.

When your order arrives

  • Fresh wasabi is perishable. Please open your package and refrigerate fresh products as soon as they arrive.
  • Please make sure someone can receive the package on the delivery day, especially in very hot or very cold weather.
  • Double-check your shipping address at checkout. We cannot change an address once an order has shipped, and we are not responsible for packages sent to an address entered incorrectly.

Damaged, delayed or lost packages

If your order arrives damaged or spoiled, or does not arrive, please contact us as soon as possible. Our refund policy explains what we will do.

Sales tax

Where the law requires us to collect sales tax, it is added at checkout.
